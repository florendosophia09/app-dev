## Reasons Why I Love Interstellar
1. Emotional story
   *- At its heart, the movie is about Joseph Cooper's promise to return to his daughter, Murph. This grounded family dynamic anchors the massive sci-fi concepts and makes the cosmic journey feel deeply personal.*

3. High-stakes time dilation
   *- The plot turns relativity into a heartbreaking dramatic tool. Watching Cooper lose decades of his children's lives in just a few hours on Miller's planet delivers one of cinema's most memorable emotional gut-punches.*
   
5. Rewarding plot twists
   *- The screenplay weaves subtle clues throughout the early scenes on Earth that pay off in the final act. The tesseract reveal reframes the entire story and makes the film especially rewarding on a rewatch.*
